Nope Door: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what the mobile game Nope Door ("the game", "we") collects and why. Questions: mostafa.m.a.w@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Player ID: every player gets an anonymous player ID from Unity Authentication (Unity Technologies).
  • Google Play Games: only if you choose to sign in, your Google Play Games player ID is linked to your game account so your progress can follow you to other devices. We don't receive your email address or Google password.
  • Display name: your in-game name (with a #number added for uniqueness). It is shown publicly on the ranking.
  • Game progress: unlocked level, levels cleared, death counts and coins, stored with Unity Cloud Save. Your levels-cleared count is shown publicly on the ranking.
  • Advertising data: the game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and interaction data to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't collect your email, phone number, contacts, location or photos.

Service providers

  • Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in): privacy policy
  • Unity Technologies (Authentication, Cloud Save, Leaderboards): privacy policy
  • Google AdMob: privacy policy

Your choices

  • You can play without signing in to anything. Google Play sign-in is optional.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings.
  • To delete your account and data, email us from the address above with your in-game name (Settings shows it). We delete it within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
