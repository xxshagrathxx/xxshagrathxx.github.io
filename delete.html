Nope Door: Delete your data

Developer: Mostafa Wahba. App: Nope Door: 1000 Troll Levels (Google Play).

How to request deletion

  1. Open Nope Door, go to Settings, and note your player name (for example Nova#4821).
  2. Email mostafa.m.a.w@gmail.com with the subject "Nope Door data deletion" and your player name.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your player account and player ID (Unity Authentication)
  • Your display name and your entry on the public ranking
  • Your saved game progress (levels, deaths, coins) in Unity Cloud Save
  • The link to your Google Play Games account, if you signed in

What is kept

We don't keep any of your data after deletion. Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google; you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings. Uninstalling the app removes the progress stored on your device.
